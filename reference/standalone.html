Setup

Three steps, and the order matters

Nothing to install and nothing to configure first. The whole thing takes about as long as reading this section.

STEP 01

Claim a key

Enter the address you want submissions sent to and confirm it. You get an access key back immediately — no account, no password, no onboarding call.

What you get

Everything the second week of a form project needs

The first version is always easy. These are the parts that show up once real submissions start arriving.

Layered spam defense

Invisible honeypot, timing checks, per-IP and per-origin rate limits, and a score on every submission. Optional Turnstile or hCaptcha when you want a visible gate.

File uploads

Send multipart/form-data and attachments are scanned, stored, and linked in the notification — or written straight to your own bucket.

Webhooks that retry

Signed payloads with exponential backoff for 24 hours, a replay button, and a delivery log that shows you the exact response body you got back.

Autoresponders

Send a confirmation from your own domain the moment a form is submitted. Write it in Markdown, pull in any submitted field, send from an address you verified.

Redirect or AJAX

Send a redirect field for a classic thank-you page, or fetch the endpoint and handle the JSON yourself. Both paths are first-class, neither is an upgrade.

Residency & retention

Choose EU or US storage per form, set a retention window in days, and submissions delete themselves on schedule. DPA available on every paid plan.

FAQ

Straight answers

How is this different from Web3Forms?

The integration is deliberately the same shape — an access key in a hidden field, one endpoint — so migrating is a find-and-replace. What’s different is everything after delivery: a dashboard with searchable history instead of email-only, signed webhooks with a 24-hour retry window and replay, autoresponders sent from your own verified domain, and a choice of EU or US storage with per-form retention. Nothing about the name implies a blockchain, because there isn’t one.

Do I need JavaScript on the page?

No. A plain <form method="POST"> works with scripting disabled — add a redirect field and the browser lands on your thank-you page. If you’d rather stay on the page, fetch() the same endpoint and read the JSON response.

Where do submissions go, and who can read them?

To the address you verified, and to the dashboard. Data is encrypted at rest in the region you pick per form. We don’t sell it, train on it, or share it with anyone, and you can set a retention window so it deletes itself on a schedule you choose.

What happens if I go over my plan’s limit?

Submissions keep arriving. We email you at 80% and 100% of your quota, and the overage is billed at a flat rate per thousand — no throttling, no dropped submissions, no surprise upgrade. If a bot is the reason you blew past the limit, filtered spam doesn’t count toward it.

Can I move an existing form over without downtime?

Yes. Point a duplicate form at Postform first, confirm the delivery looks right, then swap the action on the live one. Keys are active the moment you verify your email, so there’s no provisioning delay in the middle of the change.

Is there an API for creating forms programmatically?

On Scale, yes — a provisioning API issues keys and sub-accounts so you can create a form per customer site. On Free and Pro you create forms in the dashboard, and there’s a read API for pulling submissions into your own systems.
